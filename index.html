Model boundaries & implementation notes

The original constant-coefficient model is y(t) = a sin(bt) + c cos(dt). Here B and D are note-relative frequency ratios, and their time-varying values are integrated into phase. This avoids the frequency error from substituting a changing frequency directly into sin(b(t)t).

The six-operator graph and effects extend the model. Four sequential amplitude rules can follow the base output law, before voice fold/drive and ADSR. This is separate from nested operator PM. “Quotient” is a bounded waveshaper. The SSB effect uses a finite Hilbert filter; it is approximate near DC and Nyquist and introduces 127 samples of delay when active.

2× and 4× modes oversample synthesis, nested rules and the trig shaper before low-pass downsampling. Ring, Hilbert shift, allpass orbit and the shared buffer bus run at the output rate and can alias at extreme settings. The FFT shows actual output; XY shows carrier branches before the output law, stack and effects. No hardware CPU or firmware-parity claim is made.

Eight software voices, per-note ADSR, six independent modulation phases, operator decay and one internal-sample delay on operator PM loops and stack feedback. Echo and reverb share output-rate buffers; the port planner is sample-memory arithmetic, not a live rate/format switch. The loop records notes, not motion or audio. WAV adds an estimated effect tail capped at 12 seconds.
